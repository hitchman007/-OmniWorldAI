# OnlyWar production parity target

## Combat
- First-person locomotion: walk, sprint, crouch, jump, mantle, slide
- ADS, hip fire, recoil, sway, reload, weapon swap, melee
- Projectile / hitscan support, armor, headshots, hit markers
- Lethal and tactical equipment
- Scorestreaks / support abilities
- Gunsmith attachments and multiple loadouts

## Modes
- Team Strike (TDM equivalent)
- Frontline (Domination / control)
- Search & Destroy
- Kill Confirmed
- Hardpoint
- Battle Royale
- Black Site Extraction (PvPvE)

## Battle Royale
- Squad / solo queues
- Loot tiers and armor
- Vehicles
- Classes
- Safe zone
- Buy stations
- Contracts
- Revive / redeploy
- Airdrops

## Extraction
- Persistent stash
- Contraband
- Contracts
- Boss encounters
- Buy stations
- Crafting / recipes
- High-risk zones
- Multiple extraction mechanisms
- Faction missions

## Meta
- Operators and cosmetic gear
- Weapon camos / blueprints
- Ranked divisions
- Profile level and stats
- Friends / parties / clans
- Seasonal Battle Pass and challenges
- Store
- Downloadable content packs
- Voice / text chat
- Controller support
- Customizable mobile HUD

## Production art requirements
This repository may not use Call of Duty maps, operator models, weapon meshes, textures, logos, audio, UI art or other protected assets. The production game requires original OnlyWar equivalents: PBR environments, rigged operators, first-person hand rigs, weapon meshes/animations, vehicles, VFX and audio.
